Community Guidelines

LinguaConnect is a friendly space for learning languages and connecting with people. Follow these guidelines to keep our community safe.

1. Be Respectful

Treat every member with kindness and respect, regardless of their language or background.

2. Support Other Learners

Help beginners improve their language skills and encourage others to participate.

3. No Harassment or Bullying

Do not threaten, insult or bully other members. Maintain a friendly environment.

4. Respect Privacy

Do not share another member's personal information without their permission.

5. No Spam

Avoid sending unwanted messages or advertisements to other members.

6. Report Inappropriate Behaviour

Report users who violate community guidelines so administrators can review the issue.
